New: frame interpolation for the MPV engine (30/48/60/120 fps), a key-free
film catalogue matched by code, and a warning before you push the volume or
the brightness too far.

matched film keeps its own poster and synopsis.

Fixed: the transport buttons read previous chapter, back 10 s, play, forward
10 s, next. The Player settings section is back on Fire OS, so you can pick
the engine on the stick.
