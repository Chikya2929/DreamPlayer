0.5.5

New: a key-free film catalogue that matches by catalogue code, and a warning
before you push the volume past 100% or the brightness past 80%.

Improved: the Get info and Fix match dialogs search the key-free catalogues,
and a matched film keeps its own poster and synopsis.

Fixed: the transport buttons in the player read previous chapter, back 10 s,
play, forward 10 s, next. The Player settings section is back on Fire OS, so
you can pick the playback engine on the stick.
